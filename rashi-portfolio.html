01About

I'm a backend-leaning full-stack developer working mostly in the MERN stack, with PostgreSQL and Redis for anything that needs to be fast or durable. Most of what I build starts from a real annoyance — a slow redirect, a manual task triage step — and turns into a small production-shaped system: auth, data model, deploy pipeline, all of it.

Lately that's meant integrating LLMs into ordinary developer workflows rather than treating AI as its own separate feature — task triage that reads a ticket and suggests labels, a resume tool that reasons about fit instead of just keyword-matching. I like backend work because the constraints are honest: it either holds up under load or it doesn't.
